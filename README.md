#EMAAR Executive Development Portfolio & Strategic Intelligence Platform

An executive intelligence platform built for luxury real estate organizations, enabling leadership teams to monitor portfolio performance, market trends, sales, construction progress, investment opportunities, and business forecasts through a unified interactive dashboard.

##  Features

- Executive Command Center with real-time KPIs
- Portfolio & Development Explorer
- Market Intelligence Dashboard
- Investor Analytics
- Sales Performance Analytics
- Construction Progress Monitoring
- Demand Forecasting
- Investment Opportunity Engine
- Strategic Recommendations
- Interactive Charts & Visualizations

## 📊 Key Metrics

- Portfolio Value
- Annual Revenue
- Portfolio ROI
- Active Developments
- Units Sold
- Construction Progress
- Luxury Demand Index
- Revenue Forecast
- Investor Demand
- Sales Velocity

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript (ES6)
- Chart.js
- GSAP
- Responsive Design

## 🎯 Business Objective

To demonstrate how executive dashboards can transform complex real estate data into actionable insights, supporting strategic decision-making across portfolio management, sales, investments, and market intelligence.

## 💼 Use Cases

- Executive Reporting
- Business Intelligence
- Portfolio Analytics
- Real Estate Performance Monitoring
- Investment Analysis
- Strategic Planning
